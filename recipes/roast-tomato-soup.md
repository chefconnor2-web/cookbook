## Ingredients

- 1.2 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- Salt and black pepper
- Small bunch of basil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the tomatoes, onion and garlic with the oil on a tray and season.
2. Roast for 35–40 minutes until soft and catching at the edges.
3. Squeeze the garlic from its skins and tip everything into a pan with the stock. Simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth, adjust the seasoning, and serve topped with torn basil.

## Notes

The soup freezes well for up to three months.
