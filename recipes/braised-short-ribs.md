## Ingredients

- 2 kg bone-in short ribs
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 onions, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 750 ml red wine
- 500 ml beef stock
- 3 thyme sprigs and 2 bay leaves

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160°C (320°F). Season the ribs well and brown them in the oil in a heavy casserole. Set aside.
2. Soften the vegetables in the same pot for 10 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste.
3. Add the wine and reduce by half. Return the ribs, add the stock and herbs, and bring to a simmer.
4. Cover and braise in the oven for 3 hours, until the meat is falling from the bone.
5. Skim the fat, reduce the sauce on the stove if needed, and serve.
